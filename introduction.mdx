RampSpec is a planned testing and observability platform for repeatable Stellar anchor conformance and interoperability runs.

<Info>
  The runtime repositories do not yet have tagged releases. This documentation
  is in the `next` channel and does not claim a live deployment.
</Info>

RampSpec tests the boundaries between Stellar anchor endpoints, wallets, identity services, payment flows, runners, and ledger evidence. It is designed to produce a reproducible conformance result, an evidence report, or a verified run. It is not an official Stellar Development Foundation certification service, a regulatory review, a security audit, or a custody system.

![RampSpec architecture showing the browser, API and workers, isolated runners, target services, Stellar networks, and evidence storage](/assets/diagrams/architecture.svg)

## End-to-end flow

1. An organization selects a target, network, asset policy, and authorized runner.
2. RampSpec discovers the target's published SEP information and records the pinned source snapshot.
3. A locked rule and scenario suite selects passive checks, stateful journeys, interactive browser work, or evidence-only verification.
4. The backend dispatches work to a public sandbox or outbound-only local runner with explicit credentials and network limits.
5. Workers execute protocol requests, callbacks, browser steps, and authorized Stellar testnet actions while recording redacted evidence.
6. The workflow correlates off-chain records with Horizon or RPC observations, applies rule classifications, and records indeterminate infrastructure states separately from failures.
7. RampSpec emits a canonical report with a hash and optional signature. A future Soroban publisher may commit a compact integrity record without placing raw traffic or identity data on-chain.
