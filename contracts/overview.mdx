RampSpec contracts are planned Soroban interfaces for compact evidence commitments and controlled authentication fixtures.

## Status

No contract specification, WASM hash, network address, or deployment manifest is released yet. Contract pages will be generated from tagged artifacts rather than copied from implementation prose.

The [contract storage reference](/reference/contract-storage) records the documentation boundary until the contracts repository publishes its first compatible release.
