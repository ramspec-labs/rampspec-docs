RampSpec contracts provide a Soroban evidence registry and controlled authentication fixtures. Production evidence contracts and test fixtures are separate classifications and are labeled in every deployment record.

## Status

Release `v0.2.1` publishes reproducible WASM, generated specifications and bindings, and an RPC-verified Stellar testnet deployment manifest. The evidence registry is available for testnet evaluation; the policy-account and web-auth contracts are test-only fixtures.

| Policy account    | `CCI45FLB47JHLHZF4Y4EQC3JPQRT7NQQAXIEZHE6GCDYCAXYPPALFMZ5` | Test-only fixture         |
| Web auth          | `CDS5V7X45CXGUVYAYXEWS4MCPUZRNKSFVMTJ5QYWNE2U4TGRV77ITHO6` | Test-only fixture         |

The [v0.2.1 release](https://github.com/rampspec-labs/rampspec-contracts/releases/tag/v0.2.1) includes `deployments/testnet/manifest.v0.2.0.json`, which binds those IDs to the canonical WASM hashes and source revision. The [contract storage reference](/reference/contract-storage) defines the verification and privacy boundary.
