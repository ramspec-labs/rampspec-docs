A RampSpec case study is a participant-approved account of a bounded pilot. It preserves method, limitations, and evidence links while exposing only information the participant authorized for publication.

<Warning>
  There are no RampSpec case studies. Empty pilot slots, private discussions,
  planned integrations, and internal checks must not be described as customer,
  partner, user, adoption, or impact evidence.
</Warning>

## Separate permissions

Collect explicit decisions for each item; one approval does not imply another:

| Permission            | Choices                                                             |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Participation record  | Private identity, authorized contacts, retention, deletion          |
| Organization identity | Named, anonymized, or no publication                                |
| Individual identity   | Named, role only, anonymized, or no publication                     |
| Quotation             | Exact approved text, attribution, placement, and expiry             |
| Logo and marks        | Exact asset, placement, size, duration, and withdrawal route        |
| Technical evidence    | Approved report IDs, excerpts, screenshots, and redactions          |
| Metrics               | Approved definitions, values, denominators, ranges, and limitations |
| Relationship wording  | Approved description that does not imply endorsement or adoption    |

The participant can authorize technical results while declining names, quotes, logos, or relationship wording. Record the permission version, signer authority, date, expiry, and withdrawal consequences.

## Anonymization choices

Anonymization removes direct identity and reduces indirect identification through corridor, geography, asset, traffic, dates, unique architecture, screenshots, error messages, and quotations. Use a stable participant alias only when longitudinal comparison requires it. Aggregate or range small metrics when exact values could identify the participant.

Document what was removed, generalized, or aggregated and who reviewed re-identification risk. Do not call material anonymous when RampSpec or a likely reader can readily map it to the participant. Restricted source evidence may remain access controlled while the public case study exposes its digest and verification custodian.

## Required case-study structure

1. **Status and permission:** publication date, participant naming choice, authorization record, and expiry.
2. **Question and scope:** product/docs versions, environment, target class, journeys, dates, participants, and exclusions.
3. **Baseline and method:** task definition, fixtures, measurement rules, sample size, support interventions, and known confounders.
4. **Observed results:** verified defect dispositions, task-time observations, completion outcomes, and linked redacted evidence.
5. **Changes:** released fixes or documentation updates with tags and regression results; planned fixes remain planned.
6. **Limitations:** what the sample, environment, and method cannot establish.
7. **Participant-approved statement:** optional exact wording, clearly attributed within its permission.
8. **Verification and refresh:** evidence custodian, reproduction route, last review, next review, and correction contact.

## Claims language

Use “participated in a bounded pilot” only when the signed record permits it. Use “the pilot observed” for results. Name the numerator, denominator, unit, environment, and time window for every metric.

Do not say `customer`, `partner`, `adopted`, `production user`, `certified`, `compliant`, `secure`, `approved by SDF`, `ecosystem-wide`, or `saves X%` unless the exact statement is separately supported and authorized. Never convert interest, a letter, repository activity, testnet execution, or one successful session into adoption.

## Review and corrections

Before publication, the participant, pilot owner, product reviewer, technical owner, security/privacy reviewer, and documentation maintainer approve the final rendered page and evidence set. Confirm all links, redactions, version labels, alt text, responsive layout, and search snippets.

Corrections preserve the prior publication date and explain what changed. Withdraw identity, marks, or quotations according to the permission record while preserving a minimally necessary audit note when lawful. A withdrawn case study cannot continue supplying a funding or adoption claim.

## Current exit status

The template and review policy are implemented. There are zero authorized case studies and zero public pilot outcomes. The item exit remains blocked until two teams complete reproducible pilots and authorize specific public evidence under this policy.
