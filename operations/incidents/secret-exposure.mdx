## Trigger and impact

Trigger on secret canary, credential in source/log/trace/artifact/browser/command/support channel, unauthorized provider read/use, unexpected authentication, or suspected operator/runner compromise. Impact depends on purpose, privileges, destinations, network, tenants, and exposure window.

## Containment

Do not copy the value. Restrict incident access, disable/revoke secret version and dependent sessions/tokens/jobs/schedules/integrations, stop risky signing/pubnet/evidence actions, quarantine contaminated objects, preserve value-free fingerprints/provider audits, and notify owner/security/privacy.

## Recovery

Rotate upstream credential/key and all derived tokens, switch verified consumers, destroy old version, remove source copies from active systems/caches while preserving required restricted evidence, rebuild compromised runners/images if needed, assess unauthorized actions, and delete or hold affected artifacts under policy.

## Verification

Prove old credential fails at every consumer/provider, new one has minimum purpose/environment/tenant scope, no active lease/cache/session remains, fingerprint/canary scan covers repositories/history/logs/traces/objects/backups/exports, actions/transactions are reconciled, and redaction/prevention tests pass.

## Communication, owner, and exercise

Secret owner and security lead coordinate with privacy/legal and affected service/target owners. Updates identify purpose/scope/time and user action without the value or sensitive endpoints. Exercise quarterly across one backend and one local-runner provider, and after provider changes; status: **not exercised**.
