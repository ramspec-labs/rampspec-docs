## Trigger and impact

Trigger on code/spec/manifest hash mismatch, unauthorized/disabled attestor, wrong commitment/count/status/network, unknown/failed duplicate transaction, event/index divergence, TTL risk, admin/key compromise, unsafe upgrade, or incorrect public claim. On-chain history may be permanent even when wrong.

## Containment

Disable evidence publication and attestor signer access, pause contract writes when authorized/safe, preserve reads/revocation, activate pubnet/network gate, freeze upgrade/admin/TTL changes, record verified ledger/transaction/event/state/code-hash evidence, and stop public display as verified on mismatch.

## Recovery

Verify release/deployment/code/network, reconcile transactions and report digests, rotate/disable compromised attestor/admin credentials, revoke or supersede incorrect records with controlled reason hash, repair/rebuild index from events, restore TTL maintenance, and use reviewed rollback only if storage-compatible; otherwise forward-fix/new deployment.

## Verification

Verify live code/schema/admin/pause/attestors, report/signature versus commitment, events/state/index continuity, active/superseded/revoked invariants, no duplicate/cycle, TTL/resource budget, old credential denial, public links/disclaimer, and local/testnet regression suite.

## Communication, owner, and exercise

Contracts/security owner leads with evidence/report/privacy/legal/product. Advisory identifies network/contract/code hash/evidence and transaction IDs, affected report digests/status, permanence, verifier/user action, and replacement/revocation without private preimages. Exercise semiannually and before/after contract upgrade; status: **not exercised**.
