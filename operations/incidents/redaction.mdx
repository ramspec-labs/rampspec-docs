## Trigger and impact

Trigger on prohibited-data/canary finding, raw secret/PII/private target in artifact/report/export/log/trace/screenshot/notification/publication, redaction-policy mismatch, or scanner/filter failure. Impact may include tenant/customer exposure and invalid evidence/public commitments.

## Containment

Stop affected capture/upload/report/export/share/telemetry pipeline, revoke URLs/grants/caches and contaminated credentials, quarantine exact objects under privacy role, disable public indexing/notifications/evidence publication, preserve safe digests/lineage/access logs, and identify downstream derivatives/recipients/backups.

## Recovery

Fix and version redaction/scanner/schema, reprocess only from authorized source or rerun with synthetic data, create new report/export IDs rather than mutate signed evidence, delete contaminated copies or apply legal hold, rotate secrets, notify recipients to delete, and supersede/revoke on-chain reference when policy requires.

## Verification

Scan source and all artifacts/derivatives/caches/CDN/search/notifications/telemetry/support/backups, prove access denial/deletion or hold, validate exact/transformed/nested/image/archive canaries, verify new report/signature/digest, review public/partner downloads, and confirm no raw fallback path.

## Communication, owner, and exercise

Privacy lead commands with security, data owner, report/runner/observability and legal. Notices state data class/time/recipients/actions without reproducing data; follow contractual/regulatory deadlines. Exercise quarterly for HTTP, screenshot, trace, export, and public-share cases; status: **not exercised**.
