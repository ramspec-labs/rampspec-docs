## Trigger and impact

Trigger on ownership/policy bypass attempts, high destination/domain/port fan-out, SSRF/rebinding/redirect signal, quota evasion, credential stuffing, malicious fixture/scenario, target complaint, unexpected traffic/value, or platform saturation. Impact includes third-party harm, data/secret exposure, cost, and availability loss.

## Containment

Activate global/tenant/target/mode kill switch, stop leases/schedules/integrations, revoke actor/service/runner credentials, block destinations/egress/IP/account safely, retain rate/policy/network/audit evidence, protect cleanup/report reads, and contact target/security/legal as severity requires. Avoid broad blocking that hides an active compromise.

## Recovery

Verify target ownership and actor history, enumerate destinations/actions/value/data, reconcile target/ledger side effects, correct auth/policy/SSRF/rate/runner controls, clean fixtures, restore capacity with stricter quotas, and re-enable only after the owner approves again. Do not convert blocked scans into findings about the target.

## Verification

Run ownership expiry/challenge, two-tenant quota, IPv4/IPv6/private/metadata/redirect/rebinding, port/scheme, destination mutation, schedule/parallel/burst, runner egress, pubnet/value, and kill-switch tests; confirm complaint/action closure and no queued retry.

## Communication, owner, and exercise

Abuse/security owner leads with platform, target owner, privacy/legal/support. State observed sources/destinations/actions/window/containment and contact path without enabling bypass or exposing targets. Exercise quarterly and after ownership/policy/egress changes; status: **not exercised**.
