## Trigger and impact

Trigger on cross-tenant object response, identifier/enumeration leak, wrong organization in job/cache/event/artifact/check/webhook, row-policy failure, unauthorized access alert, or mismatched audit context. Treat potential data/control exposure as security-critical until bounded.

## Containment

Disable affected endpoint/worker/cache/integration/share path, revoke relevant sessions/service/runner credentials, stop queues and cache reads/writes in scope, preserve access/query/object/job/audit digests, apply broad read-only mode if scope is unknown, and prevent logs/support from exposing another tenant.

## Recovery

Identify authorization/data-layer root cause and all resources/tenants/time/actors, purge or partition caches, cancel/move misrouted jobs without replay, correct ownership keys/policies, rotate exposed secrets/links, restore objects/rows from verified source if mutated, and deploy reviewed fix with no insecure compatibility fallback.

## Verification

Run exhaustive two-tenant read/write/list/search/cache/job/SSE/artifact/report/integration/runner tests, direct database/object policy checks, audit correlation, negative ID enumeration, affected access review, deletion/rotation, and synthetic end-to-end runs for both tenants.

## Communication, owner, and exercise

Security incident commander and authorization owner lead with privacy/legal/data/product. Notify affected organizations separately with confirmed data/actions/time/user steps; never reveal another tenant's identity/data. Exercise quarterly and after auth/data/cache changes; status: **not exercised**.
