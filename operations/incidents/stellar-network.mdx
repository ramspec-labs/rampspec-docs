## Trigger and impact

Trigger on Horizon/RPC availability/latency/errors, stale/divergent ledger, wrong passphrase/network, malformed XDR, simulation/submission uncertainty, ingestion gap/reorganization, or provider compromise. Ledger correlation/evidence may be delayed or untrusted; target results become infrastructure-indeterminate where required.

## Containment

Stop new submissions/evidence writes and affected workflows/schedules, activate pubnet kill switch when relevant, remove suspect endpoint from allowlist, preserve request/response digests/ledger/provider/release context, and prevent automatic failover across network/passphrase. Continue verified report reads.

## Recovery

Confirm network health from independent trusted sources/endpoints, verify passphrase/genesis/provider identity and latest finalized ledger, reconcile every unknown transaction by hash/source sequence/state, restore from reviewed endpoint/config, backfill bounded ledger range, and resume observation before simulation/submission.

## Verification

Compare independent ledger headers/transactions/events, validate XDR/network/code hash, prove no duplicate submission, fill index gaps without duplicate events, run synthetic testnet observe/simulate/submit under caps, and confirm failure classifications/reports/objectives.

## Communication, owner, and exercise

Stellar/platform owner leads with protocol/evidence/security and target owners. Updates distinguish provider, network, RampSpec, and target impact; state networks/ledgers/transactions safely, submission disablement, and user retry guidance. Exercise semiannually and on provider/SDK/network config changes; status: **not exercised**.
